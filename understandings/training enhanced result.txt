Training Results Summary

The Stage 2 training was conducted over 30 epochs using mixed precision (AMP) and gradient accumulation (effective batch size = 32). The model was trained on a large dataset with 5190 batches per epoch, and the training process was stable across all epochs with a gradually decreasing learning rate schedule.

1. Training Stability and Convergence
The training loss consistently decreased from 0.0110 → 0.0025, showing strong convergence.
The optimization process remained stable without divergence or oscillations.
Learning rate scheduling progressively reduced the learning rate from 1e-4 to near 0, enabling fine-tuning in later epochs.
2. Validation Performance Evolution

The model was evaluated regularly using standard image captioning metrics:

CIDEr (primary metric): improved steadily from 4.11 → 9.88 (best)
BLEU-4: improved from 8.47 → ~15.30 peak, then stabilized around ~13
ROUGE-L: improved from 30.39 → ~39.35 peak, stabilizing around ~36–37
METEOR: remained near 0 (likely due to implementation or tokenization limitations)
3. Best Model Performance
The best checkpoint was achieved at Epoch 12
Corresponding results:
CIDEr: 9.88 (best score)
BLEU-4: 15.30
ROUGE-L: 39.35

After this point, performance slightly fluctuated but did not significantly improve further, indicating convergence.

4. Qualitative Observations
Early epochs produced highly generic captions.
Mid-training outputs became more structured and relevant to aerial scenes (e.g., airports, runways, buildings).
Later epochs showed some regression in linguistic quality, with repetitive or less coherent phrases despite stable loss values.
5. Overall Assessment
The model successfully learned meaningful visual representations of remote sensing scenes.
It demonstrates strong improvement in semantic alignment between images and captions.
However, the plateau after Epoch 12 suggests:
possible overfitting,
or limited capacity of the language generation head,
or need for stronger decoding / regularization strategies.

🟦 Early Training (Epoch 2–4) — Weak semantic alignment

At this stage, captions are mostly generic and poorly grounded in the image content:

Ground Truth (example):

“the tarmac and airport runways divide the field into several orderly arranged rounded rectangles next to which is buildings and a road”

Model output:

“many buildings are near a airport where outside in another area northwest of the near a nearby airport outside where but some buildings”

➡️ Observation:

Repetitive phrases (“near a airport”, “outside”)
Weak spatial reasoning
Incorrect object relationships
🟨 Mid Training (Epoch 8–12) — Emerging structure

Captions start to reflect correct scene concepts (airports, planes, buildings), but remain noisy:

Ground Truth:

airport runways, buildings, parking area

Model output:

“many planes are near a terminal in an airport nearby where another outside but they so even not because those these where but”

➡️ Observation:

Correct domain understanding (airport, planes, terminal)
Still grammatically broken
High redundancy and hallucinated words
🟩 Best Performance Phase (Epoch 12–16)

This is where your model performs best in CIDEr.

Ground Truth:

airport with runways, buildings, parking lot

Model output:

“many planes are in an airport near some buildings and green trees parking few these those many planes some airplanes are near the”

➡️ Observation:

Strong semantic grounding
Correct detection of key objects (planes, airport, buildings)
Still lacks fluent sentence structure
🟧 Late Training (Epoch 20–30) — Slight degradation / drift

Even though loss keeps decreasing, text quality becomes less stable:

Ground Truth:

airport runways, buildings, road

Model output:

“some planes are near an airport nearby village another a large building a one an airport a another but only this area near”

➡️ Observation:

More fragmented sentences
Repetition increases again
Slight semantic drift (“village”, “large building” introduced incorrectly)
